# Yeni parçalar 3: Uğurlama ve Filiz (23 Kasım 2026)

Geri bildirimden sonraki tur. Bohça, Sandık, Çatma ve Yaka tutmadı: düz tek renk gövdeye tek motif koymak zorlanmış bir minimalizm gibi durdu. Bağ beğenildi. Bu turun kuralı şu:

1. **Okunaklı, heykelsi bir form.**
2. **Bütün gövdeyi saran tek ve düzenli bir süs sistemi.** Kırk Kat'ın katları ya da Bağ'ın kanalları ve kuşağı gibi. Gelişigüzel süs yok.
3. **Sistem bir hikâye anlatır.** Süsün sırası hikâyenin sırasıdır.

Prompt'lar ChatGPT için: her birini **yeni bir sohbette tek başına** gönder.

**Önerim: önce Uğurlama.** Markanın yol hikâyesine en doğrudan bağlanan parça bu. Senin beğendiğin iki şeyi birleştiriyor: Bağ'daki saran kuşak ve M2'deki kilim deseni.

---

## Uğurlama · berjer

- **Hikâye:** Anadolu'da yola çıkanın arkasından su dökülür: "su gibi git, su gibi gel". Kilimde her motif bir dilektir. Koltuk, yolcuya söylenen bir uğurlama cümlesi gibi okunur:
  - Sol kolun önü: **koçboynuzu**, güç ve cesaret, yola çıkış.
  - Sonra: **kurt izi**, yolda koruma.
  - Sırtın ortası: büyük **yıldız**, mutluluk ve gece yolunu gösteren ışık.
  - Sağ kolun önü: **bereket**, bolluk içinde varış.
  - Dizinin altında ve üstünde: altın **su yolu** bordürleri, yolcunun arkasından dökülen su; yolun kendisi.
- **Form:** Alttaki kalın, yuvarlak bir oturum davulunun üstüne at nalı biçiminde çok kalın bir rulo oturur; sırtı ve kolları tek kıvrımla yapar. İki yumuşak hacim, uzaktan net okunan bir silüet. Bilinen iskelet: yuvarlak davul puf + rulo sırt; döşemeci sünger ve basit bir iskeletle kurar. Rulonun dış yüzü silindir olduğu için nakış kırışmaz.
- **Süs sistemi:** Rulonun bütün dış yüzünde ton sür ton, kabartmalı (3D köpüklü) kilim frizi. Kilim motifleri dar sütunlardan oluştuğu için köpüklü nakışa en uygun desen (bkz. 6. bölüm). Bu büyüklükte ve kabartmalıyken ton sür ton parlar.
- **İmzalar:** Nakış frizin kendisi. Pirinç damga sırttaki yıldızın altında, davulun üstünde. Püskül damgadan sarkar.
- **Risk:** Tek renk kırmızı fazla sakin gelirse, motifleri lacivert aplike yapan düzeltme prompt'u aşağıda.

```
Generate a photorealistic landscape image, 3:2 aspect ratio:

Two photographs side by side of the same contemporary sculptural lounge chair, a piece of collectible design with a bold, legible silhouette. It is made of two soft, generous volumes. Below, a thick round seat drum, 42 cm tall, with a deep plain seat cushion. On top of it, wrapping around the back and both sides, a very thick horseshoe-shaped roll, about 34 cm in diameter, that forms the back and the arms in one continuous curve, like a huge bolster laid around the seat, its two ends rounded off at the front. A low recessed dark plinth hidden in shadow so it seems to float. Contemporary and sculptural, but not minimal: the whole outer surface carries one composition.

Fabric: deep madder-red cotton velvet throughout.

The composition, which tells a story: an Anatolian farewell to a traveller, written in the old language of kilim motifs, where every motif is a wish. It runs along the whole outer face of the horseshoe roll, from the front of the left arm, around the back, to the front of the right arm, as one continuous frieze that fills the roll from top to bottom:
- at the front of the left arm, rows of ram's-horn motifs: strength and courage to set out;
- then rows of wolf's-track motifs, small hooked diamonds: protection on the road;
- at the centre of the back, one large eight-pointed star flanked by two smaller stars: happiness and a light through the night;
- at the front of the right arm, the abundance motif, stepped diamonds filled with smaller diamonds and hooks: a rich arrival.
The motifs are large, bold and geometric, with crisp stepped edges like a woven kilim, and flow into each other without gaps. They are worked as raised, padded satin-stitch embroidery in glossy madder-red silk, exactly the same red as the velvet, so they read through relief and sheen, like carved stone. Dense but calm and ordered: one family of motifs, one rhythm.

The road: along the lower edge of the roll, and again around the top edge of the seat drum, run two continuous "water path" meander borders, 4 cm tall, drawn in antique-gold cord couched by hand, like the water poured behind a departing traveller. They frame the whole frieze and are the only gold lines.

The brass and the tassel: on the outside of the drum, directly below the large star at the centre of the back, one small solid polished brass plate, about 6 cm wide, set flush into the velvet and engraved with a small, simple abstract geometric mark of our own (not any existing tribal symbol). From it hangs a single tassel, 12 cm long, in madder-red silk with a gold-wrapped head. It is the only metal and the only tassel.

Nothing else: no buttons, no tufting, no piping, no fringe, no other colours.

Left: front three-quarter view, showing the ram's horns on the left arm, the abundance motifs on the right arm and the deep seat. Right: back three-quarter view, showing the large star, the gold meanders and the brass plate with its tassel.

Width 100 cm, depth 90 cm, seat height 42 cm, overall height 76 cm. Warm grey studio backdrop, soft key light from the upper left, soft floor shadow, 50 mm lens, f/8. A real photograph of a real, buildable piece of contemporary collectible design. No text, no people in the scene.
```

## Filiz · berjer

- **Hikâye:** Hayat ağacı: kökü yerde, dalları gökte. Koltuğun kendisi bir ağaç. Kanal dikişlerindeki altın kordonlar tabandan gövde gibi yükselir, yarı yükseklikte dallanır, kenarda nar ve hatayi çiçeklerinden bir taç olur. Tohum aşağıda (pirinç damga), meyve yukarıda (püskül).
- **Form:** Kadeh biçimi: dar, yuvarlak bir tabandan yukarı doğru açılan, kenarı geniş bir gövde. Sırt ortada yüksek, kollara doğru iner. Dikey kanallar (Bağ'da beğendiğin yapı) tabanda dar, kenarda geniş. Uzaktan bir kadeh ya da açan bir tomurcuk gibi okunur.
- **Süs sistemi:** Yapıdan doğuyor: her kanal dikişi zaten bir çizgi, o çizgi altın kordonla ağacın gövdesine dönüşüyor. Kırk Kat'taki altın çizgi mantığının dikey ve büyüyen hâli. Sırtın yaslanan iç yüzünde yalnızca altın dikiş çizgileri var, kabartma yok.
- **İmzalar:** Taç nakışı (altın ve kabartmalı kırmızı narlar). Pirinç damga tabanda, bütün altın gövdelerin çıktığı noktada: tohum. Püskül sırtın tepesinde, en büyük narın altından sarkar: meyve.
- **Risk:** Taç fazla kalabalıklaşırsa "deli gibi süs"e kayar. Prompt tacı yalnızca üst bandla sınırlıyor; yine de taşarsa aşağıdaki düzeltmeyi kullan.

```
Generate a photorealistic landscape image, 3:2 aspect ratio:

Two photographs side by side of the same contemporary sculptural armchair, a piece of collectible design with a bold, legible silhouette shaped like a goblet: a narrow round base, about 55 cm across, from which the body flares outward and upward to a wide round rim, about 95 cm across. The rim is highest at the centre of the back and sweeps down smoothly towards the front, where it forms the arms. Inside, a deep, round seat with one plain cushion. It stands on a low recessed dark round plinth hidden in shadow so it seems to float. Contemporary and sculptural, but not minimal.

The body: the whole wall, inside and outside, is made of plump vertical channels in plain deep moss-green cotton velvet, narrow at the base and widening towards the rim, about 9 cm wide at the top. The seat cushion is the same green velvet.

The composition, which tells a story: the chair is a tree of life, with its roots below and its crown above. In every seam between the channels, on the outside, a fine antique-gold cord is couched by hand. At the base, all the cords start together like the trunk of a tree. They rise straight up the seams; from about half the height of the wall, they begin to branch: fine gold tendrils leave the seams and curl out over the channels into split-leaf rumi leaves. In the top band of the outer wall, about 20 cm deep, all around the chair, the branches bloom into a crown of pomegranates and hatayi blossoms: the pomegranates in raised, padded satin stitch of madder-red silk with fine gold seeds, the blossoms and leaves in antique-gold thread. The crown is densest and largest at the centre of the back and becomes lighter towards the front of the arms. Below the crown the green velvet stays calm, with only the rising gold lines. Ordered and elegant: one tree, one rhythm, nothing scattered. Not a cypress, no birds, no figures.

Inside: on the inside of the wall, where the back rests, only the plain gold seam cords, with no branches and no raised embroidery.

The brass and the tassel: at the foot of the outside back, exactly where the gold cords start together, one small solid polished brass disc, about 6 cm across, set flush, engraved with a small, simple abstract geometric mark of our own (not any existing tribal symbol): the seed of the tree. At the top of the back, from the rim just below the largest pomegranate, hangs a single tassel, 12 cm long, in madder-red silk with a gold-wrapped head, like a ripe fruit. The disc is the only metal and the tassel is the only tassel.

Nothing else: no buttons, no tufting, no piping, no fringe, no other embroidery.

Left: front three-quarter view, showing the goblet silhouette, the deep seat and the crown on the arms. Right: back three-quarter view, showing the gold lines rising from the brass seed, branching, and blooming into the crown, with the tassel at the top.

Width 95 cm, depth 90 cm, seat height 40 cm, back height 84 cm at the centre, 64 cm at the arms. Warm grey studio backdrop, soft key light from the upper left, soft floor shadow, 50 mm lens, f/8. A real photograph of a real, buildable piece of contemporary collectible design. No text, no people in the scene.
```

## Düzeltme prompt'ları (aynı sohbette, her turda bir tane)

**Uğurlama tek renk sönük kalırsa:**
```
Edit this image. Keep the form, the gold meanders, the brass plate and the tassel exactly as they are. Change only the kilim motifs on the roll: make them flat appliqué of deep navy-blue velvet on the red, each outlined with a fine antique-gold cord, instead of tone-on-tone relief.
```

**Uğurlama'nın rulosu ince ya da ayrı yastık gibi çıkarsa:**
```
Edit this image. Keep everything exactly as it is. Change only the back: it is one single, very thick, continuous horseshoe roll, fixed to the drum, not loose cushions.
```

**Filiz'in tacı kalabalıklaşırsa ya da aşağı taşarsa:**
```
Edit this image. Keep everything exactly as it is. Change only the crown: keep it within the top 20 cm of the outer wall, with fewer, larger pomegranates and more green velvet showing between them; below it only the rising gold lines and a few branching tendrils.
```
