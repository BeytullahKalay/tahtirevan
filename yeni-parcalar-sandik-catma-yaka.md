## Sandık · sandıklı bench

- **Hikâye:** Çeyiz sandığı, gelinle birlikte yeni eve yolculuk eden ve en değerli kumaşları (bohçaları) saklayan nesnedir. Bohça ile aynı aile: ikisi birlikte "çeyiz" ikilisi olur.
- **Form:** Döşemeli, kapaklı, saklama alanlı bench; iç mimarların yatak ucu için en çok istediği parça. Bilinen iskelet olduğu gibi kalıyor; yeniden yorum, kapağın altındaki friz ve gerçekten kilitlenen pirinç kilit.
- **İmzalar:**
  - **Nakış:** Kapağın hemen altında, sandıkların oymalı ön frizi gibi tam boy bir nakış şeridi.
  - **Pirinç damga:** Gerçek anahtar deliği olan kilit aynası; damga aynanın üstünde. Pirinç, elin değdiği yerde.
  - **Püskül:** Kilitteki pirinç anahtardan sarkar. Anahtar püskülü gerçek bir gelenek.
- **Sürpriz:** Kapak açılınca içi kök boya kırmızısı kadife; çeyizin içi gibi.
- **Risk:** Sandık fazla "antika" okunursa, frizi sadeleştir; form zaten modern.

```
Generate a photorealistic landscape image, 3:2 aspect ratio:

Two photographs side by side of the same contemporary upholstered storage bench, a piece of collectible design, inspired by the Anatolian dowry chest that travelled with a bride to her new home: a solid rectangular chest 120 cm long, 48 cm deep and 46 cm tall, with crisp, softly rounded edges, sitting on a low recessed dark plinth hidden in shadow so it seems to float. Its lid is a firm upholstered seat, 10 cm thick, overhanging the body by 1 cm all round and hinged at the back.

Fabric: the body and the lid are deep forest-green cotton velvet, plain.

The hero: on the front face, directly under the lid like the carved frieze under the lid of an old chest, an embroidered band 14 cm tall runs the full length of the bench. On it, a dense, elegant continuous scroll of Anatolian ornament: fine spiralling stems carrying split-leaf rumi motifs and small palmettes, with one larger palmette at the centre as the axis, worked in antique-gold thread, slightly raised, fine and precise, on the green velvet. Its lower edge is a plain tonal seam.

The brass, the key and the tassel: at the centre of the front face, just below the band, one solid polished brass lock plate, about 9 cm wide, a softly rounded rectangle with a real keyhole, engraved with a small, simple abstract geometric mark of our own (not any existing tribal symbol). In the lock sits a small brass key, and from the key hangs a single slim tassel, 12 cm long, in madder-red silk with a gold-wrapped head. The lock plate and its key are the only metal; it is the only tassel.

Inside: the inside of the chest and the underside of the lid are lined in plain madder-red velvet.

Nothing else: no buttons, no tufting, no piping, no handles, no corner fittings, no nailheads, no other embroidery.

Left: front three-quarter view with the lid closed, showing the band, the lock and the tassel. Right: the same bench with the lid lifted open, showing the madder-red lining inside.

Length 120 cm, depth 48 cm, height 46 cm. Warm grey studio backdrop, soft key light from the upper left, soft floor shadow, 50 mm lens, f/8. A real photograph of a real, buildable piece of contemporary collectible design. No text, no people in the scene.
```

## Çatma · berjer

- **Hikâye:** Bursa çatma kadifesinde tüy bazı yerlerde kesilip boşaltılır, alttaki altın ya da metal zemin görünür; desen bu boşluktan doğar. Biz bunu döşemeye taşıyoruz: dış sırttaki kadifeden tek bir büyük motif oyuluyor, altından mat altın ipek görünüyor.
- **Form:** Yüksek, dik bir yarım silindir sırt (sütun kesiti gibi), önünde alçak ve derin blok oturum ve blok kollar. Sırt tek yönde kıvrıldığı için nakış paneli kırışmaz (kural 12). Ön yüz sade, hikâye arkada.
- **İmzalar:**
  - **Nakış:** Oyuğun kenarı yoğun altın saten dikişle kapanır; oyuğun içinde, altın zeminin üstüne kırmızı iplikle ince iç çizgiler (nar taneleri, yaprak damarları).
  - **Pirinç damga:** Dış sırtın sağ alt köşesinde, kaidenin hemen üstünde küçük bir levha.
  - **Püskül:** Levhadan sarkar.
- **Üretim:** Oyma aplike (ters aplike), makine nakışında bilinen bir teknik: kadife lazerle kesilir, kenarı saten dikişle kapatılır. 60 cm'lik motif tek kasnağa sığmaz; ZSK gibi 140 cm'lik büyük kasnak ya da modüllere bölme gerekir. Bu yüzden üçünün en pahalısı.
- **Risk:** Kırmızı ve altın "saray" gibi okunabilir. Altını mat tut; gerekirse kırmızıyı koyu yeşille değiştir.

```
Generate a photorealistic landscape image, 3:2 aspect ratio:

Two photographs side by side of the same contemporary armchair, a piece of collectible design with a commanding presence: a tall, upright back shaped as a half-cylinder, like a section of a column, standing behind a low, deep block seat with one plain seat cushion and two low, solid block arms with flat faces. It sits on a low recessed dark plinth hidden in shadow so it seems to float. Modern and architectural: no wings, no rolled arms, no visible legs, nothing classic.

Fabric: the whole chair is deep crimson cotton velvet, plain.

The hero, inspired by the çatma velvets of Bursa, where the velvet pile is cut away so that the gold ground beneath shows through: on the outside of the curved back, one single large motif, a Bursa pomegranate palmette about 60 cm tall, a pointed pomegranate framed by curling split leaves, is cut out of the crimson velvet, and through this opening a ground of matte antique-gold silk shows, set slightly back. The cut edge of the velvet is bound all round with a dense, smooth satin stitch in antique-gold thread. Inside the opening, on the gold silk, fine inner lines, the seeds of the pomegranate and the veins of the leaves, are embroidered in crimson thread. The motif is centred on the back. Everything else on the chair is plain velvet.

The brass and the tassel: in the lower right corner of the outside of the back, just above the plinth, one small solid polished brass plate, about 6 cm wide, set flush into the velvet and engraved with a small, simple abstract geometric mark of our own (not any existing tribal symbol). From it hangs a single slim tassel, 12 cm long, in crimson silk with a gold-wrapped head. It is the only metal and the only tassel.

Nothing else: no buttons, no tufting, no piping, no fringe, no other embroidery, no other metal. The gold is matte and antique, not shiny palace gilt.

Left: front three-quarter view, showing the plain crimson front, the seat and the arms. Right: back three-quarter view, showing the cut-out pomegranate with its gold ground, the brass plate and the tassel.

Width 88 cm, depth 84 cm, seat height 40 cm, back height 98 cm, arm height 58 cm. Warm grey studio backdrop, soft key light from the upper left, soft floor shadow, 50 mm lens, f/8. A real photograph of a real, buildable piece of contemporary collectible design. No text, no people in the scene.
```

## Yaka · berjer (önerim)

- **Hikâye:** Osmanlı kaftanları Bursa kadifelerinden dikilirdi; önü çapraz kavuşur, kenarda dış kumaşı tamamlayan renkte astar görünürdü. Terzinin ustalığı yakada okunur. Koltuğu bir kaftan gibi "giydiriyoruz", ama kostüm yapmadan: yalnızca çapraz kavuşma, astar ve kenar şeridi alınıyor.
- **Form:** Kalın kollu, düz yüzlü, modern küp klub koltuk (hazır iskelet). Dış sırt ve yanlar tek bir kumaşla sarılmış; dış sırtta sol panel sağın üstüne çapraz biner. Kavuşma kenarı 3 cm kabarık durur, gölge yapar: süs, yapının kendisi.
- **İmzalar:**
  - **Nakış:** Kavuşma kenarında dönen 6 cm'lik safran astar şeridi boyunca ince altın hatayi çiçekleri (tek başına nakış şeridi).
  - **Pirinç damga:** Şeridin alt ucunda, kavuşmayı kapatan kısa pirinç çubuk düğme (toka); damga çubuğun üstünde.
  - **Püskül:** Çubuğu tutan altın kordon ilmeğinin ucunda. Damga ile tek nesne.
- **Neden ucuz:** İskelet hazır. İş, döşemecinin dış kumaşı çapraz bindirmesi ve tek düz bir nakış şeridi (makine nakışı, modüllere bölünebilir).
- **Risk:** Yapay zekâ kavuşmayı bağcık ya da korse gibi çizebilir; prompt bunu yasaklıyor ("corset" kelimesi bilerek kullanılmadı).

```
Generate a photorealistic landscape image, 3:2 aspect ratio:

Two photographs side by side of the same contemporary club armchair, a piece of collectible design with a commanding presence: a generous, modern cubic form with a deep seat, one plain seat cushion, a straight back and thick arms with flat front faces; full, solid volumes with softly rounded edges. It sits on a low recessed dark plinth hidden in shadow so it seems to float. No wings, no rolled arms, no visible legs, nothing classic.

Fabric: the whole chair is upholstered in deep olive-green cotton velvet, plain.

The idea, taken from an Ottoman kaftan whose front crosses over and shows its lining at the edge: the outside of the back is dressed like a robe. Two panels of the olive velvet meet there, and the left panel crosses over the right one with one long, straight diagonal edge running from the top left corner of the back down to the bottom right corner, just above the plinth. The overlapping edge stands about 3 cm proud of the panel beneath and casts a soft, clean shadow line. Along this edge the velvet is turned back to show a 6 cm band of its lining, a saffron-ochre silk velvet, like the edge of a robe. Smooth and tailored: no lacing, no straps, no buttons along the edge.

The hero: the saffron band carries a dense, fine continuous scroll of small Ottoman hatayi blossoms and leaves embroidered in antique-gold thread, slightly raised, running the whole length of the diagonal edge. It is the only embroidery on the chair.

The brass and the tassel: at the lower end of the diagonal, where the edge meets the bottom right corner, the overlap is closed with one solid polished brass toggle, a short rounded bar about 7 cm long, held by a loop of antique-gold cord, and engraved with a small, simple abstract geometric mark of our own (not any existing tribal symbol). The cord loop ends in a single slim tassel, 12 cm long, in saffron silk with a gold-wrapped head. It is the only metal and the only tassel.

The front of the chair, the inside of the back, the arms and the seat are plain olive velvet: calm and modern.

Nothing else: no buttons, no tufting, no piping, no fringe, no other embroidery, no other metal.

Left: front three-quarter view, showing the calm olive front and a glimpse of the saffron edge on the back. Right: back three-quarter view, showing the full diagonal overlap with its embroidered saffron band, the brass toggle and the tassel.

Width 94 cm, depth 88 cm, seat height 40 cm, height 78 cm. Warm grey studio backdrop, soft key light from the upper left, soft floor shadow, 50 mm lens, f/8. A real photograph of a real, buildable piece of contemporary collectible design. No text, no people in the scene.
```
